Design is not just how something looks. It is how it thinks.

My work sits at the intersection of design, technology, systems and human experience. I care about understanding the problem before deciding what the solution should look like.

The goal is not simply to make something visually impressive. The goal is to create something that makes sense, works well and has a reason to exist.
