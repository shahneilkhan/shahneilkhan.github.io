01 / BRAND STORY

ONE IDENTITY. MULTIPLE DIMENSIONS.

Software, AI, product and UX may look like separate disciplines. For SNK, they are connected parts of the same engineering mindset.

SNK represents how I approach digital work: engineering with curiosity, designing with intention and building toward meaningful outcomes.

The brand is deliberately minimal. It should feel technical without becoming cold, premium without becoming excessive, and intelligent without relying on generic futuristic visuals.
